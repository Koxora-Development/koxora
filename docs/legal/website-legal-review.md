# Website pre-publication legal review checklist

**Status: prepared content for review, 2026-10-24, updated after the independent review. This checklist is not legal advice and is not a statement of compliance.** It records what the Website W1 implementation actually does, which statements were taken from Product-Owner-supplied reference material, and what the operator must verify against the deployed services before `koxora.app` is published. Implementation alone cannot establish lawfulness; a qualified legal review of the published text and the real deployment is required first.

Related: [Issue #26](https://github.com/Koxora-Development/koxora/issues/26), [Issue #21](https://github.com/Koxora-Development/koxora/issues/21), [ADR 0017](../adr/0017-public-website-delivery.md), [W1 implementation notes](../platform/website-w1.md).

## 8. Open points before publication

1. Confirm the c/o address may be used for the Koxora brand and that the operator details are current.
2. Create `security@koxora.app`, run inbound and outbound delivery tests for all four mailboxes, and assign an owner to each. The security address is published in the legal notice, the footer, `SECURITY.md` and `/.well-known/security.txt`, so it must receive mail before the site goes live.
3. Confirm the tax statement and the competent supervisory authority.
4. Confirm the processing agreements with the hosting, delivery and email providers, and whether the delivery network is actually in front of the published site.
5. Measure and record the real log retention period, then replace the placeholder wording with the measured value.
6. Re-run the device storage audit against the deployed configuration and keep the result with the release record.
7. Resolve the supervisory-authority question so that the named authority and the printed address agree.
8. Check the outbound links before publication, as the legal notice states.
9. Obtain a professional legal review of both language versions.
10. Decide and publish the source code licence, because the legal notice refers to it.
